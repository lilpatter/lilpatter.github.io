About

Finds the latest Game Ready and Studio drivers for your NVIDIA card, checks they're genuine and installs them with one prompt.

Features

  • Matches your exact card with NVIDIA's catalog
  • Only runs installers signed by NVIDIA
  • Express or custom install, with roll back
  • Live GPU temperature, load and VRAM
