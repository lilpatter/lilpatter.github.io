About

Finds your RPCS3 and Lichii games and every save, starts any game straight from its library, and edits the saves of supported games with each game's own artwork.

Features

  • RPCS3 and Lichii in one library
  • Play without the emulator's game list
  • Save editors for LEGO Batman and LEGO Indiana Jones
  • A backup before every change
