How They’re Built

  • Light on your PCHangar trims what an idle Steam holds in RAM from about 330 MB to 30–70 MB. Prism uses about 4 MB of memory in the tray. Parley has no Electron and no browser engine. Memory figures are measured on Windows 11 with the app idle; the Under the Hood notes on each app page say exactly what was measured.
  • Your accounts stay yoursHangar never reads or stores a password: it signs in with a QR code from your phone, and the token it gets back is encrypted on your PC.
  • Careful with your systemVerdant only runs installers signed by NVIDIA. Keepsake makes a backup before every change. Prism injects nothing into your games and needs no admin rights.
  • Straight from GitHubEvery download comes from the app’s GitHub Releases page, and each app page shows the version and the date it was last updated.

About

PG is one developer making the apps I want on my own PC. Most replace a heavy official app with something smaller, or do the one thing the official app leaves out.

The apps are closed source for now. Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on the app’s GitHub page.
