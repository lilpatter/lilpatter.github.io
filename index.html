About

PG is one developer making the apps I want on my own PC. Most replace a heavy official app with something smaller, or do the one thing the official app leaves out. They’re free and closed source for now, and every download comes from GitHub Releases with its SHA-256 hash on the app’s page.

Found a bug or have an idea? Open an issue on the app’s GitHub page. If an app saves you some time, you can buy me a coffee.
