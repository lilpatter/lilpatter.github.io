Lobby

Switch Steam accounts. Silently.

A small native app for Steam that changes the signed-in account without the account picker or a password prompt, and shows every game on every account.

Available for Windows
Download for Windows View source

Silent switching.

Steam restarts straight to the tray on the account you chose, and Lobby confirms the switch.

Play as…

A game owned by another account on this PC is one click away: switch and launch together.

Your whole library.

Installed and not installed, with Steam's own cover art, playtime, last played and favorites.

Never touches your password.

Lobby never reads or stores passwords. Steam keeps its own remembered sign-in.

Driver Updater

Your GeForce driver, always current.

Finds the latest Game Ready and Studio drivers for your card, downloads them, checks they're genuine and installs with one prompt.

Available for Windows
Download for Windows View source

Knows your card.

Detects your GPU, installed driver and whether you're on a desktop or laptop, then asks NVIDIA for the latest release.

Only genuine installers.

Every download's signature is checked. Only files signed by NVIDIA Corporation will run.

Express or custom.

Resumable downloads, then one UAC prompt for a silent install, or NVIDIA's own installer if you prefer.

Live GPU readings.

Temperature, load, VRAM, power, clock and fan, plus release history with roll back for both channels.
