About

A native app for iPhone, iPad, Mac and Apple TV. Discover what to watch, keep your place across devices, and play from the sources you already use.

Features

  • Home, arranged your way
  • Built-in player, or the one you prefer
  • Your place synced with Trakt and CataSync
  • Media servers and Stremio add-ons
