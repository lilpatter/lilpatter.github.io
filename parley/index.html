About

A small native Discord client for Windows: servers, channels, DMs, replies, emoji and voice calls end-to-end encrypted with DAVE, Discord's own protocol for calls, in a single exe with no Electron and no browser engine. Text messages aren't end-to-end encrypted, in Parley or in any Discord client.

Why Parley Exists

Discord's own app is built on Electron: a full copy of the Chromium browser, running in several processes even while it waits in the tray.

Parley does the everyday parts of Discord (servers, DMs and voice calls) in a small native exe instead. It says plainly what it leaves out and what using an unofficial client risks, so you can decide whether it's for you.

Features

  • Servers, channels and DMs
  • Voice calls end-to-end encrypted with DAVE
  • Replies, emoji and invite cards
  • Sign in by scanning a QR code
  • Stays signed in across restarts
  • No Electron and no browser engine

Under the Hood

  • Stays signed inA failed first request after boot, before the network is up, no longer signs you out. Parley retries with backoff and only drops an account when Discord says its session is no longer valid. Saved accounts are written atomically, with a backup copy.
  • Lighter by subtractionScreen sharing, camera, the bundled video library and the diagnostics tools were cut, taking the exe from 93 MB to 42 MB. With its window open and idle, Parley trims its own working set (the part of its memory held in RAM) to around 7 MB. The memory it has allocated in total is larger.
  • Careful updatesParley updates itself from its GitHub releases and checks each download's SHA-256 hash first. It waits until you're not busy, gives you ten seconds to say Later, then updates quietly in the tray.

Privacy & Security

What it connects to

  • Discord: everything a Discord client does: sign-in, messages and voice
  • hCaptcha: only if Discord asks for a captcha while you sign in
  • GitHub: to check for and download updates

What stays on your PC

  • Your Discord session is encrypted with Windows' own data protection (DPAPI) and never leaves your PC except to talk to Discord
  • Settings live in %APPDATA%\Parley
  • Voice calls are end-to-end encrypted with DAVE, Discord's protocol for calls. Text messages are encrypted on their way to Discord but not end to end, the same as in Discord's own app
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports, and nothing is ever sent to PG.

Downloads and updates

  • Checks its GitHub Releases page for updates, verifies each download against its SHA-256 hash, and asks before installing.
  • The downloads aren’t code-signed yet, so Windows may say the publisher is unknown. To check a download is the real one, compare its SHA-256 hash with the ones shown here.
