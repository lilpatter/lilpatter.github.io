Parley

An unofficial, lightweight Discord client for Windows.

Download for Windows Available

For Windows 10 and 11. There’s no Mac version yet.

Unofficial client: use at your own risk

Parley isn't made or approved by Discord. Discord's Terms of Service forbid “unauthorized software designed to modify the services”, and Discord can suspend or close accounts that break its terms. Discord can also change its service at any time, which may stop Parley working until it's updated. Screen sharing and camera aren't supported. Don't use it on an account you can't afford to lose.

About

A small native Discord client for Windows: servers, channels, DMs, replies, emoji and voice calls with Discord's own end-to-end encryption, in a single exe with no Electron and no browser engine.

Features

  • Servers, DMs and encrypted voice calls
  • Replies, emoji and invite cards
  • Sign in by scanning a QR code
  • Stays signed in across restarts
  • No Electron and no browser engine
  • One light exe, idle in the tray

Under the Hood

  • Stays signed inA failed first request after boot, before the network is up, no longer signs you out. Parley retries with backoff and only drops an account when Discord says its session is no longer valid. Saved accounts are written atomically, with a backup copy.
  • Lighter by subtractionScreen sharing, camera, the bundled video library and the diagnostics tools were cut, taking the exe from 93 MB to 42 MB. The window idles at around 7 MB of memory.
  • Careful updatesParley updates itself from its GitHub releases and checks each download's SHA-256 hash first. It waits until you're not busy, gives you ten seconds to say Later, then updates quietly in the tray.

Privacy & Security

What it connects to

  • Discord: everything a Discord client does: sign-in, messages and voice
  • hCaptcha: only if Discord asks for a captcha while you sign in
  • GitHub: to check for and download updates

What stays on your PC

  • Your Discord session is encrypted with Windows' own data protection (DPAPI) and never leaves your PC except to talk to Discord
  • Settings live in %APPDATA%\Parley
  • No analytics, telemetry or crash reports, and nothing is ever sent to PG.

Downloads and updates

  • Checks its GitHub Releases page for updates, verifies each download against its SHA-256 hash, and asks before installing.
  • The installers aren’t code-signed yet, so Windows may say the publisher is unknown. To check a download is the real one, compare its SHA-256 hash with the one shown here.

Requirements

  • Windows 10 or 11, 64-bit
  • A Discord account you're willing to risk (see the warning above)
  • WebView2, only for the captcha window
